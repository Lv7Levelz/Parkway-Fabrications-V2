# Research, content and SEO strategy

## Research record and constraint

Research was attempted on 27 September 2026 against the live domain and web search. The execution environment returned HTTP 403 for the live site and HTTP 401 from the search provider. Consequently, no inaccessible statement, legacy URL, search volume, competitor claim or Google review has been presented as verified. The supplied brief is the only factual source used in the build. A pre-launch audit must rerun the work with access to Google Search, Search Console, the live CMS and the verified Google Business Profile.

No Google reviews are published. The future review model must store exact reviewer name, rating, verbatim review text, review date and source URL, and must require approval before display. Review schema is deliberately absent.

## Creative concept: “The resolved drawing”

The visual system takes its cues from an engineering drawing without imitating CAD software. Overscale, tightly set typography supplies confidence; construction lines, datum labels, part geometry and a single safety-orange accent communicate precision. Warm off-white and deep green-black avoid the predictable black/neon industrial treatment. No synthetic factory, machinery, staff or project imagery is used.

The central proposition is “From drawing to finished part.” It connects the buyer's input to Parkway's joined-up processes without claiming an unsupported technical outcome.

This structure separates materially different search and buyer intents, but avoids doorway pages and artificial locations. Each service page contains a distinct H1, title, description, useful process copy, internal links, breadcrumb and Service JSON-LD.

## Homepage and conversion strategy

1. Immediate proposition, drawing upload and telephone route.
2. Three verified equipment facts.
3. Connected-process explanation.
4. Scan-friendly capability index.
5. Dual entry point: finished drawing or unresolved problem.
6. Published materials with an availability caveat.
7. physical Sheffield location and direct contact.
8. persistent, decisive footer quote CTA.

The RFQ uses progressive sections—contact, requirement and drawings—with practical fields and room for uncertainty. Service links preselect the relevant capability. Analytics hooks exist for quote start/submission, drawing count, phone, email and service CTAs; a consent-reviewed GA4 or privacy-focused alternative can consume the data layer in production.

## Content and image strategy

Copy is short, buyer-led and explicit about what is confirmed at technical review. Genuine workshop, machinery and completed-component photography should eventually be commissioned with a shot list: the building exterior; identifiable 6kW laser; 3m bed; Edwards Pearson press brake; operator hands without staged portraits; material surfaces; verified finished parts; and process sequences. Capture landscape, portrait and detail variants with written usage permission. Until then, original inline technical geometry is safer and more credible than stock or generated factory imagery.

## Facts requiring Parkway confirmation

- The correct monitored public enquiry email; none is published until confirmed.
- Legal entity name, company number, VAT number and data controller contact.
- Founding date, years trading, team size and geographic service area.
- Exact laser model, supported sheet dimensions/material-thickness limits and cutting tolerances.
- Press-brake material/thickness limits and achievable tolerances.
- Welding processes, coded-welder qualifications and applicable standards.
- Finishing processes performed in-house versus by partners.
- Delivery/collection coverage, typical lead-time policy and opening hours.
- Quality, environmental or health-and-safety certifications.
- Granulator and shredder models supported, screen specification process and terminology.
- Genuine projects, customer permissions, outcomes, images and client logos.
- Current Google Business Profile URL and an export of exact current reviews.

## Deliberate omissions

No review score, testimonials, founding year, certifications, tolerances, thickness limits, named customers, case studies, guarantees, turnaround promises, machine imagery or map embed appears because none could be verified. FAQ schema is omitted because it no longer warrants routine rich-result treatment and the release has no sufficiently useful approved FAQs.
